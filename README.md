# Enrico Giuliano

**Pharmacist · Founder & Product Lead of [SOPHIA](https://github.com/EG2025-lab/sophia-pharmacy) · Italy**

I am building SOPHIA to help pharmacists navigate official medicines documentation, Italian pharmaceutical legislation, clinical evidence and guidelines in one professional conversation. Our goals are to reduce information fragmentation and workload, help reduce errors and improve pharmacy service. Their impact still needs to be measured.

FarmAI is available as original custom GPTs and private Business plugins derived from their copies. SOPHIA is a separate application under development, with its own restricted beta. The public repository shares documentation, evaluation proposals and collaboration opportunities; application source code is not published there.

The deployed professional beta is **0.7.121-beta**, restricted to owner-approved users; payments and answer charging remain inactive. [Current status](https://github.com/EG2025-lab/sophia-pharmacy/blob/main/docs/STATUS.md).

Ten new natural counter questions were submitted once each on **120**. Ten final responses were delivered: **six useful within the internally assessed scope, two partial and two without the requested facts**. No engine timeout was observed. A safe abstention or delivered response is not a complete answer PASS. Full transcripts and primary-source comparisons are retained; this is not human clinical validation.

No further real question was submitted after the ten. No own **121** real-answer acceptance, general repeatability, controlled speed/cost improvement, FarmAI parity, SLA or human clinical validation is established. A balanced updated magnesium evidence comparison and ordinary partial dispensing remain open. Technical source access does not prove legal currency, personal entitlement or applicability. Historical reports/packages remain intact; FarmAI GPTs and Business plugins retain content **0.7.7.155**.
