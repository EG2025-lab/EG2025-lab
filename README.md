# Enrico Giuliano

**Pharmacist · Founder & Product Lead of [SOPHIA](https://github.com/EG2025-lab/sophia-pharmacy) · Italy**

I am building SOPHIA to help pharmacists navigate official medicines documentation, Italian pharmaceutical legislation, clinical evidence and guidelines in one professional conversation. Our goals are to reduce information fragmentation and workload, help reduce errors and improve pharmacy service. Their impact still needs to be measured.

FarmAI is available as original custom GPTs and private Business plugins derived from their copies. SOPHIA is a separate application under development, with its own restricted beta. The public repository shares documentation, evaluation proposals and collaboration opportunities; application source code is not published there.

The deployed professional beta is **0.7.124-beta**, restricted to owner-approved users; payments and answer charging remain inactive. [Current status](https://github.com/EG2025-lab/sophia-pharmacy/blob/main/docs/STATUS.md).

Privacy policy **20** broadens professional-language admission across questions, generated output, history, feedback, reviews and sharing. On **1,266 synthetic filter cases**, all **1,170 admissible cases** pass and **96 adverse cases** remain blocked; 122 admitted 168 of those same 1,170 cases. This deliberately selected family set is not a general privacy or accuracy benchmark. Public product codes, product names and attributed documentary quotations receive distinct handling; actual personal identifiers and individual therapeutic-purpose checks remain.

Exactly **four new real AIFA inputs** were submitted once each **after 124 publication, on the same frozen version**. All four passed admission. Three final answers were delivered: **two useful within documentary scope (one with declared official web-original recovery), one partial**, and **one generated-text privacy rejection without a final answer**. The discarded draft was not retained, so its exact phrase and whether the output rejection was a false positive remain unknown. **1,914 offline checks, 58 interface checks and 195 image runtime files** passed qualification. Fourteen current official AIFA PDFs were independently reread. Delivered-answer engine times were 78.23, 29.71 and 76.74 seconds; the failed fourth request stopped after about 34.30 seconds. Different examples and no matched live baseline establish no causal speed or cost gain.

Post-battery candidate **125** additionally distinguishes administration-route terminology from street addresses and recognises more public-code label spellings. **1,920 offline checks and 58 interface checks** pass; **125 is not deployed, has zero own real questions and remains NO-GO for own real acceptance**. These grammar refinements do not prove resolution of the discarded fourth draft. Rocefin structured branch isolation, incomplete paired reading and repetitive limitation text remain open. Report124 and RC125 are separately sealed; no fifth question. No general FarmAI parity, professional rollout, SLA or human clinical validation. FarmAI GPTs and Business plugins retain content **0.7.7.155**.
